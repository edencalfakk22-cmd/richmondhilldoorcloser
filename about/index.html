About Elgin Mills Door Hardware Co.

Elgin Mills Door Hardware Co. works on the part of the door most people never look at until it fails. Closers, exit devices, hold-opens, and the pivots and strikes that decide whether a door latches: it is a narrow trade, and doing only that is what lets us walk into a Richmond Hill condo tower, look at forty fire doors in an afternoon, and know which ones need a valve turned and which ones need the unit off the wall.

Almost all of our work is commercial, institutional and multi-residential — property managers with fire doors that have to stand up to an inspection, plaza landlords along Yonge, Highway 7 and Elgin Mills whose tenant entrances take salt and grit all winter, Beaver Creek office condominiums, and schools and community centres with exit hardware and accessible operators to keep running. We are insured and WSIB-covered, we do not invent problems to sell hardware, and when a closer only needs adjusting we say so and adjust it.

The other half of the job is being honest about the season. Half the emergency calls we take in January are doors that had been drifting quietly out of adjustment since November. We would rather do one scheduled pass in the fall than three panic visits in the cold, and every job — planned or urgent — comes with a written, fixed-scope estimate before anyone picks up a screwdriver.

  • Fire doors handled building-wide, with a written record of what was corrected
  • Seasonal adjustment for York Region cold, stack pressure and humid summers

Insured & WSIB-covered

We carry full liability insurance and WSIB coverage, and we service closers and exit hardware so fire doors close and latch on their own, the way Ontario’s Fire Code requires — serving property owners across Richmond Hill and the Greater Toronto Area.
